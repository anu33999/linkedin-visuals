## ❌ Don't post these without written permission from the NGO

- Everything in `5-agape/` except the `photos-blurred-*` files. That includes `scroll-video.mp4`. They show the faces of children who live in an HIV/AIDS care home.
- Everything in `6-helping-hands-humanity/` except the `photos-blurred-*` files. They show real children, and the README says some photos were cropped to remove watermarks.

## Tips

- Videos: LinkedIn plays them muted, which is fine because these have no sound.
- PDF carousel: choose **Add a document**, upload the PDF, and give it a title like "One website. Six languages."
- `desktop-home-fullpage.png` files are very tall. Use them for your portfolio or GitHub README, not LinkedIn.
